# A New Era for Urban Planning

By 2050, nearly 7 out of 10 people will live in cities (UN-Habitat). This means billions more residents will depend on planners to deliver housing, infrastructure, transport, and green space. But as cities expand, the complexity of zoning rules, environmental regulations, and urban policy frameworks increases dramatically.

Traditional planning methods — thick zoning books, static maps, and endless reports — no longer match the speed of urban change. Residents struggle to understand how policies affect their neighborhoods, developers face uncertainty, and governments risk delays.

AIRIlab steps into this gap. Built as an AI platform for the built environment, it extends its architecture-first intelligence into urban-scale planning. The result? Zoning and policy visualizations that make sense to everyone — from policymakers to citizens.
