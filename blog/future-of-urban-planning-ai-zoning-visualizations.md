## Why Zoning and Policy Are Difficult to Visualize

Urban planning has always suffered from a communication challenge. The issue is not accuracy, but accessibility.

**Key challenges include:**

- **Policies are text-heavy**  
  Zoning reforms often span hundreds of pages. While legally precise, they remain inaccessible to residents, community groups, and non-specialist stakeholders.

- **Maps lack experiential context**  
  A 2D zoning map shows boundaries, but cannot explain how a mid-rise building affects **sunlight, views, wind flow, or pedestrian comfort**.

- **Scenario modeling is slow**  
  Testing changes—such as higher FAR, reduced parking minimums, or new green corridors—often requires days or weeks of manual modeling.

The outcome is a disconnect: **cities plan decades of growth without a clear way to show what those rules actually create.**
